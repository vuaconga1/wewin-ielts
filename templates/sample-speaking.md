# Sample Speaking

## Topic: Part 1 - Hometown

Where is your hometown?
What do you like about it?
Has it changed much in recent years?

## Topic: Part 2 - Describe a book

Describe a book you enjoyed reading.
You should say:
- what the book was
- when you read it
- what it was about
and explain why you enjoyed it.

## Topic: Part 3 - Reading habits

Do people read less nowadays?
Should schools require students to read more fiction?
